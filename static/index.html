Interactive Tooltip

Trade-Off Analysis & Operational Frontier

In high-throughput enterprise intrusion detection, selecting an operating point requires balancing alert fatigue against catastrophic breach exposure:

  • Baseline (τ = 0.41): FPR = 2.84%, Recall = 67.85%. Leaves 32.15% of attacks unflagged due to default probability thresholds.
  • Final Balanced (τ* = 0.10): FPR = 3.42%, Recall = 78.27%. Achieves the optimal cost-sensitive balance under a 5:1 FN:FP misclassification penalty.
  • High Detection (τ = 0.06): FPR = 7.63%, Recall = 81.42%. Captures maximal intrusions at the expense of a 2.2x increase in false alarms.
